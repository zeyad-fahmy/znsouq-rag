# Shipping Orders Yourself (Fulfilled by Seller)

## Handling time
You must ship within **3 business days** of the order, or within the handling time shown on your listing (maximum 5 business days, for example for made-to-order items).

## Tracking
Every shipment must have valid tracking from a ZNSouq-integrated courier, uploaded within 24 hours of shipping. Your **valid tracking rate** must stay above 95.0%.

## Shipping fees
You set your own shipping fees per zone. You may offer free shipping. ZNSouq's delivery zones (A-D) are used for your shipping templates.

## Cash on delivery
You may offer COD for seller-fulfilled orders if your courier supports it. Cash collected is reconciled to your ZNSouq balance within 5 business days of delivery; you never collect payment directly.

## Delivery promise
If you miss the latest estimated delivery date, the order counts as **late**. Buyers can file a ZNSouq Guarantee claim 3 days after the latest estimated delivery date.
