# Account Health

Account Health measures how well you serve buyers. Metrics are calculated over a rolling 60-day window unless noted.

| Metric | Target | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Order Defect Rate (ODR) | below 1.0% | Orders with negative feedback (1-2 stars), a ZNSouq Guarantee claim, or a chargeback |
| Late Shipment Rate | below 4.0% | Seller-fulfilled orders shipped after the handling time (30-day window) |
| Pre-fulfillment Cancellation Rate | below 2.5% | Orders cancelled by the seller before shipping (7-day window) |
| Valid Tracking Rate | above 95.0% | Seller-fulfilled shipments with valid tracking (30-day window) |
| Response Time | 90.0% of messages answered within 24 hours | Buyer messages (business days only) |

FBZ orders do not count toward late shipment or tracking metrics.

## What happens if you miss a target
1. **Warning**: you receive a notification and have 14 days to improve.
2. **At risk**: listings may lose Buy Box eligibility.
3. **Deactivation**: if the ODR stays above 1.0% or the cancellation rate stays above 2.5% after a warning, your account can be deactivated. See "Suspension and Appeals".

Policy violations (counterfeit, review manipulation, off-platform payments) can lead to immediate deactivation without a warning.
