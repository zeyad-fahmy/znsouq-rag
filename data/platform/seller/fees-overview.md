# Seller Fees Overview

## Referral fees
A referral fee is charged on every item sold, as a percentage of the **total sale price** (item price + any shipping charged to the buyer), with a minimum of 5 EGP per item.

| Category | Fee until 31 Oct 2026 | Fee from 2026-11-01 |
|---|---|---|
| Electronics | 8% | 9% (changed) |
| Mobile Phones & Tablets | 6% | 7% (changed) |
| Computers & Accessories | 7% | 7% |
| Home Appliances | 9% | 9% |
| Home & Kitchen | 12% | 12% |
| Men's Fashion | 15% | 14% (changed) |
| Women's Fashion | 15% | 14% (changed) |
| Kids & Baby | 11% | 11% |
| Beauty & Personal Care | 13% | 13% |
| Health & Nutrition | 12% | 12% |
| Sports & Outdoors | 12% | 12% |
| Toys & Games | 12% | 12% |
| Books | 10% | 10% |
| Grocery | 7% | 7% |

See "Referral Fee Changes - November 2026" for details on the change.

## Other fees
- **Closing fee**: 10 EGP per item on the Individual plan only.
- **Plan fee**: 399 EGP/month on the Professional plan.
- **FBZ fees**: fulfillment, storage, long-term storage and removal (see "FBZ Fees").
- **Refund administration fee**: when you refund a buyer, ZNSouq refunds you the referral fee minus an administration fee of 20% of that referral fee (max 50 EGP per item).
- **Advertising**: cost per click, set by your bids.
- **VAT**: 14% is added to all ZNSouq fees. A monthly tax invoice is issued.

## Example
A Men's Fashion shirt sold for 450 EGP with free shipping, Professional plan, seller-fulfilled, sold on 25 October 2026:
- Referral fee: 15% x 450 = 67.50 EGP
- VAT on fee: 14% x 67.50 = 9.45 EGP
- Total fees: 76.95 EGP; you receive 373.05 EGP.
The same sale on or after 2026-11-01 has a 14% referral fee: 63.00 EGP + 8.82 EGP VAT = 71.82 EGP.
