# Seller Tiers

| Tier | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Standard | all new sellers |
| Rising Star | at least 100 orders in the last 30 days, store rating 4.5+, and all account health metrics in good standing |
| Top Seller | at least 1,000 orders in the last 90 days, store rating 4.7+, ODR below 0.5%, and late shipment rate below 2% |

## Benefits
- **Rising Star**: Buy Box eligibility before 90 days of history, a "Rising Star" badge, and a 10% discount on Lightning Deal fees.
- **Top Seller**: "Top Seller" badge, a dedicated account manager, 20% discount on Lightning Deal fees, and payouts with a 3-day reserve instead of 7 days.

Tiers are re-evaluated on the 1st of every month.
