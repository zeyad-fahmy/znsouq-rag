# Seller Fees: Men's Fashion

- **Referral fee**: 15% of the total sale price, minimum 5 EGP per item.
- **Changes on 2026-11-01**: 15% -> 14% (based on order date).
- **Gated category**: No
- **Minimum return window you must offer**: 30 days
- Plus the per-item closing fee (10 EGP, Individual plan only), FBZ fees if applicable, and 14% VAT on all fees.

## Example: 1,000 EGP sale
- Before 2026-11-01: referral fee 150.00 EGP + VAT 21.00 EGP = 171.00 EGP
- From 2026-11-01: referral fee 140.00 EGP + VAT 19.60 EGP = 159.60 EGP
