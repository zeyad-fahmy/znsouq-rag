# Seller Fees: Grocery

- **Referral fee**: 7% of the total sale price, minimum 5 EGP per item.
- No change scheduled.
- **Gated category**: Yes. Approval required before listing (see "Gated Categories").
- **Minimum return window you must offer**: not returnable (damaged/expired/wrong items only)
- Plus the per-item closing fee (10 EGP, Individual plan only), FBZ fees if applicable, and 14% VAT on all fees.

## Example: 1,000 EGP sale
- Before 2026-11-01: referral fee 70.00 EGP + VAT 9.80 EGP = 79.80 EGP
- From 2026-11-01: referral fee 70.00 EGP + VAT 9.80 EGP = 79.80 EGP
