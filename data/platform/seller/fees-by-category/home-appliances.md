# Seller Fees: Home Appliances

- **Referral fee**: 9% of the total sale price, minimum 5 EGP per item.
- No change scheduled.
- **Gated category**: No
- **Minimum return window you must offer**: 15 days
- Plus the per-item closing fee (10 EGP, Individual plan only), FBZ fees if applicable, and 14% VAT on all fees.

## Example: 1,000 EGP sale
- Before 2026-11-01: referral fee 90.00 EGP + VAT 12.60 EGP = 102.60 EGP
- From 2026-11-01: referral fee 90.00 EGP + VAT 12.60 EGP = 102.60 EGP
