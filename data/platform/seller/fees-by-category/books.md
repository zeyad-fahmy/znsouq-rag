# Seller Fees: Books

- **Referral fee**: 10% of the total sale price, minimum 5 EGP per item.
- No change scheduled.
- **Gated category**: No
- **Minimum return window you must offer**: 30 days
- Plus the per-item closing fee (10 EGP, Individual plan only), FBZ fees if applicable, and 14% VAT on all fees.

## Example: 1,000 EGP sale
- Before 2026-11-01: referral fee 100.00 EGP + VAT 14.00 EGP = 114.00 EGP
- From 2026-11-01: referral fee 100.00 EGP + VAT 14.00 EGP = 114.00 EGP
