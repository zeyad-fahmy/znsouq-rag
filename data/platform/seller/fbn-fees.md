# FBZ Fees

## Fulfillment fee (per unit sold)
| Size tier | Limits | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Small envelope | up to 250 g, max 30 x 20 x 2 cm | 22 EGP |
| Standard small | up to 1 kg, max 35 x 25 x 12 cm | 30 EGP |
| Standard large | up to 5 kg, max 45 x 35 x 20 cm | 45 EGP |
| Oversize | up to 20 kg, max 120 x 60 x 60 cm | 95 EGP |
| Heavy & bulky | over 20 kg or larger than oversize | 180 EGP |

Heavy & bulky: 180 EGP + 6 EGP per kg above 20 kg. Weight is the greater of actual weight and dimensional weight (L x W x H in cm / 5000).

## Monthly storage fee (per cubic foot)
- January to September: 25 EGP
- October to December: 60 EGP (peak season)

## Long-term storage
Units stored more than 180 days are charged an extra 15 EGP per cubic foot per month, assessed on the 15th of each month.

## Removal and disposal
8 EGP per unit for removal (returned to you) or disposal.

## Returns processing
No fee for buyer returns of FBZ items. Unsellable returns are moved to your "unfulfillable" inventory; you have 30 days to request removal before they are disposed of at the removal fee.

All fees are before 14% VAT.
