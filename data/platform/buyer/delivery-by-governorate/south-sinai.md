# Delivery to South Sinai

South Sinai is in **delivery Zone D**.

- Standard delivery (Fulfilled by ZNSouq): 5-8 business days, 85 EGP.
- Express delivery is not available.
- Free standard delivery threshold does not apply in Zone D; Plus members still get free standard delivery on FBZ items.
- Large and bulky items: 300 EGP, delivered within 3-7 business days.
- Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 5,000 EGP (15 EGP fee).
- Returns: drop-off at a pickup point only (no courier pickup).
- Seller-fulfilled items: add the seller's handling time (usually 1-3 business days) to the transit time; shipping fees are set by the seller.
- Delivery covers Sharm El Sheikh, Dahab and El Tor only.
