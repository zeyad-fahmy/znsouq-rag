# Delivery to Gharbia

Gharbia is in **delivery Zone B**.

- Standard delivery (Fulfilled by ZNSouq): 2-3 business days, 40 EGP.
- Express: next day (80 EGP).
- Free standard delivery on Fulfilled by ZNSouq orders of 500 EGP or more.
- Large and bulky items: 150 EGP, delivered within 3-7 business days.
- Cash on delivery is available for orders up to 20,000 EGP (15 EGP fee; free for Plus members).
- Returns: free courier pickup from your address, or drop-off at a pickup point.
- Seller-fulfilled items: add the seller's handling time (usually 1-3 business days) to the transit time; shipping fees are set by the seller.
