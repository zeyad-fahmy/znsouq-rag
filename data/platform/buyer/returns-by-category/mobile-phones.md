# Returns: Mobile Phones & Tablets

Applies to all Mobile Phones & Tablets items on ZNSouq (smartphones, tablets, chargers, phone cases), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: 15 days from delivery
- **Change-of-mind returns**: only if the device is still factory-sealed
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Report damaged or wrong items within 7 days of delivery; defects any time within the return window.
- **Default warranty**: 12 months (if the listing does not state otherwise)

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- SIM cards
- devices with the activation seal broken (unless defective)
