# Returns: Toys & Games

Applies to all Toys & Games items on ZNSouq (LEGO-style building sets, dolls, board games, RC cars), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: 30 days from delivery
- **Change-of-mind returns**: if unopened
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Report damaged or wrong items within 7 days of delivery; defects any time within the return window.
- **Default warranty**: No default warranty

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- opened puzzle or card packs
