# Returns: Automotive

Applies to all Automotive items on ZNSouq (car accessories, tires, dash cams, car care), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: 15 days from delivery
- **Change-of-mind returns**: only if unused and not installed
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Report damaged or wrong items within 7 days of delivery; defects any time within the return window.
- **Default warranty**: 6 months (if the listing does not state otherwise)

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- electrical parts once installed
