# Returns: Health & Nutrition

Applies to all Health & Nutrition items on ZNSouq (vitamins, protein powder, blood pressure monitors), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: 14 days from delivery
- **Change-of-mind returns**: only if sealed and unused
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Report damaged or wrong items within 7 days of delivery; defects any time within the return window.
- **Default warranty**: No default warranty

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- opened supplements
- medical devices once used
