# Returns: Books

Applies to all Books items on ZNSouq (novels, textbooks, children's books), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: 30 days from delivery
- **Change-of-mind returns**: if in new condition
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Report damaged or wrong items within 7 days of delivery; defects any time within the return window.
- **Default warranty**: No default warranty

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- digital codes
- magazines
