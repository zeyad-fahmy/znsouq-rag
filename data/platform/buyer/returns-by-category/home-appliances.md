# Returns: Home Appliances

Applies to all Home Appliances items on ZNSouq (refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, blenders, irons), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: 15 days from delivery
- **Change-of-mind returns**: only if unopened and not installed
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Large appliances (refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, ovens): report damage within 48 hours of delivery. Small appliances: within 7 days.
- **Default warranty**: 24 months (if the listing does not state otherwise)

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- appliances that have been installed by a technician (unless defective)
