# Returns: Women's Fashion

Applies to all Women's Fashion items on ZNSouq (dresses, abayas, shoes, handbags, jewelry), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: 30 days from delivery
- **Change-of-mind returns**: if unworn, unwashed and with original tags attached
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Report damaged or wrong items within 7 days of delivery; defects any time within the return window.
- **Default warranty**: No default warranty

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- lingerie
- swimwear with the hygiene liner removed
- earrings for pierced ears
