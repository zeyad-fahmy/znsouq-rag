# Returns: Electronics

Applies to all Electronics items on ZNSouq (TVs, headphones, speakers, cameras, smart watches), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: 15 days from delivery
- **Change-of-mind returns**: only if unopened and in original sealed packaging
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Report damaged or wrong items within 7 days of delivery; defects any time within the return window.
- **Default warranty**: 12 months (if the listing does not state otherwise)

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- software or game codes once revealed
