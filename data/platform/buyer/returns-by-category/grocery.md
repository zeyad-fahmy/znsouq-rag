# Returns: Grocery

Applies to all Grocery items on ZNSouq (coffee, snacks, rice, cooking oil, cleaning supplies), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: No change-of-mind returns. Damaged, expired or wrong items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery.
- **Change-of-mind returns**: not accepted
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Report damage or wrong items within 48 hours of delivery.
- **Default warranty**: No default warranty

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- all grocery items, except damaged, expired or wrong items reported within 48 hours of delivery
