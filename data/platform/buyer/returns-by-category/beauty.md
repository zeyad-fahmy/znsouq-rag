# Returns: Beauty & Personal Care

Applies to all Beauty & Personal Care items on ZNSouq (skincare, makeup, perfume, hair dryers, shavers), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: 14 days from delivery
- **Change-of-mind returns**: only if the product is sealed and unused
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Report damaged or wrong items within 7 days of delivery; defects any time within the return window.
- **Default warranty**: No default warranty

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- any opened cosmetics, skincare or fragrance
