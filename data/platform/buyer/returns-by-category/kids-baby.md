# Returns: Kids & Baby

Applies to all Kids & Baby items on ZNSouq (strollers, car seats, baby clothes, feeding bottles), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: 30 days from delivery
- **Change-of-mind returns**: if unused and in original packaging
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Report damaged or wrong items within 7 days of delivery; defects any time within the return window.
- **Default warranty**: 6 months (if the listing does not state otherwise)

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- opened feeding bottles and pacifiers
- opened diapers
