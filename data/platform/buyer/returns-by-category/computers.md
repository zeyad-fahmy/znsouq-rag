# Returns: Computers & Accessories

Applies to all Computers & Accessories items on ZNSouq (laptops, monitors, keyboards, storage drives, printers), from any seller. Sellers may offer more generous terms on their store page, never stricter ones.

- **Minimum return window**: 15 days from delivery
- **Change-of-mind returns**: only if unopened
- **Damaged/defective/wrong items**: Report damaged or wrong items within 7 days of delivery; defects any time within the return window.
- **Default warranty**: 12 months (if the listing does not state otherwise)

## Not returnable (unless defective, damaged or wrong)
- opened software
- ink cartridges once installed
