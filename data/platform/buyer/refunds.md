# Refunds

Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the returned item passes inspection.

| Original payment method | Refund timing |
|---|---|
| ZNSouq Wallet | within 24 hours after inspection |
| Credit/debit card | 5-10 business days after inspection, depending on your bank |
| Meeza card | 5-10 business days after inspection |
| Cash on delivery | to your ZNSouq Wallet within 24 hours, or by bank transfer within 7 business days if you request it |
| Installments (ZNPay) | the installment plan is cancelled and paid installments are refunded within 14 business days |

## What is refunded
- The item price.
- The delivery fee, **only** if the item was defective, damaged, wrong, or never delivered.
- The COD fee is not refunded, except for defective, damaged or wrong items.
- For change-of-mind returns of FBZ items, 25 EGP return shipping is deducted (not for Plus members).

## Refund not received?
Card refunds can take up to 10 business days to appear on your statement. If 10 business days have passed since the refund date shown in your order, contact customer service with the refund reference number (format RF-XXXXXXXX) so your bank can trace it.
